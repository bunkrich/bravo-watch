Clubhouse Tonight← Tonight's schedule

Bravo on your home screen

A free iPhone widget that shows the next new episodes (or Peacock drops) at a glance. Tap it to open the full schedule. It updates itself.

1. Get the free Scriptable app

Scriptable is a free, well-known iPhone app that runs small widgets like this one.

Open in the App Store

2. Copy the widget

Copied ✓
  1. Open Scriptable and tap + (top right).
  2. Paste the code, then tap the title at the top and name it Clubhouse Tonight.
  3. Tap ▶ to preview it, then Done.

3. Add it to your home screen

  1. Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap Edit → Add Widget.
  2. Search Scriptable, pick the Large (most nights) or Medium size, and tap Add Widget.
  3. Long-press the new widget → Edit Widget → set Script to Clubhouse Tonight.
  4. Set When Interacting to Open URL and URL to https://clubhousetonight.com so a tap opens the schedule.

Peacock person?

In Edit Widget, type peacock in Parameter. The widget switches to Peacock drop times (6:00 AM the morning after). You can add one of each.
